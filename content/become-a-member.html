What members do

Members of Action Sweetwater Creek Inc. hold working bees each week. These include propagation of plants indigenous to the reserve, and planting, weeding and light clearing tasks.

ASC Inc. activities also include:

  • Inviting experts to lead walks on flora, birds and edible weeds
  • Representing the community to Council about development projects
  • Protecting the reserve's indigenous integrity

If you are interested in becoming a member, please send us your details using the form below, then pay your subscription by bank transfer or in cash.
