Protecting the reserve's indigenous integrity

Action Sweetwater Creek Inc. has a membership of about 250 local residents. Our activities include:

  • Liaising with Frankston City Council and Melbourne Water
  • Distributing information to the community
  • Representing the community to Council about development projects
  • Inviting experts to lead walks on flora, birds and edible weeds

ASC Inc. holds community participation days, such as Frog Day, when local residents were encouraged to develop frog habitats.

Local schools are also encouraged to include the reserve and its wealth of natural information in their curriculum.

Ways to help

Volunteer, learn, join or give

  • Volunteer Sessions

    Every Thursday 9am to 11am and the first Saturday of the month. Equipment provided.

  • Living Classroom

    Sessions with local schools, and notes from our guided walks with experts.

  • Become a Member

    Membership is open to anyone interested in the preservation of indigenous nature reserves.

  • Support Us

    Donate by bank transfer or cash to help maintain the reserve.
