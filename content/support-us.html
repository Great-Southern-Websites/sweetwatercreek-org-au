Where your donation goes

Members of Action Sweetwater Creek Inc. hold working bees each week: propagating plants indigenous to the reserve, planting, weeding and light clearing. Your donation supports that work and the community days, school sessions and expert-led walks that go with it.

To donate, send us your details using the form below and choose how you will pay.
