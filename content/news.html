22 May 2026

Community Planting Day, Saturday 6 June

Get involved with your reserve, it's worth it. Enjoy this lovely community gathering and a Saturday morning with your family.

Action Sweetwater Creek invites you to come down to the reserve on Saturday 6 June, 10am to midday. Join us at the Hoadley Avenue entrance to Sweetwater Creek Nature Reserve.

  • We'll finish with a delicious morning tea.
  • We'll give you a plant to take home.
  • Sturdy shoes and gloves are essential.

Booking is essential, at sweetwater.action@gmail.com.
