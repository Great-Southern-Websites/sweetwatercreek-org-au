Thirteen hectares of remnant bushland

Sweetwater Creek extends from the foothills of Frankston Reservoir to Port Phillip Bay at the base of Olivers Hill. Thirteen hectares of remnant bushland surrounding the lower section of the creek is protected for its flora, fauna, geological and cultural heritage values.

Walking tracks wind their way along the creek from swamps to steep gullies and floodplains to foothills. Lookouts, seats and bridges make resting places along the way, and a circuit of the area takes about an hour.

Seen in the reserve

A field guide kept by volunteers

At least 11 varieties of native orchid and about 50 species of bird have been identified in the reserve. Our members photograph what they find while weeding and planting.

Working bees

Every Thursday, 9am to 11am, and the first Saturday of the month, 9am to 11am

Members of Action Sweetwater Creek Inc. hold working bees each week: propagating plants indigenous to the reserve, planting, weeding and light clearing. All equipment is provided, and morning tea follows the first Thursday session of each month.
