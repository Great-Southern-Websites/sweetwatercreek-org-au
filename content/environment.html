The reserve

From Frankston Reservoir to Port Phillip Bay

Sweetwater Creek extends from the foothills of Frankston Reservoir to Port Phillip Bay at the base of Olivers Hill. Thirteen hectares of remnant bushland surrounding the lower section of the creek is protected for its flora, fauna, geological and cultural heritage values.

The reserve corridor is a ribbon of forest, heath, swamp and grassland interlocked by walking trails and resting places. It adjoins the stream for its length from Kars Street to the bay, varying in width from a few metres to a couple of hundred, and is controlled and maintained by Frankston City Council.
