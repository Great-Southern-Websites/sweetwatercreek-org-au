When we meet

Every Thursday 9am to 11am
First Saturday of the month 9am to 11am
Morning tea First Thursday of each month, at the completion of the working bee

If you wish to join either group, please email us at sweetwater.action@gmail.com.

Email to join Become a member

What we do

Tasks include weeding and planting native species, with regular walks to review progress and seasonal changes.

What to bring

  • Sturdy shoes
  • Suitable clothing for the weather

All equipment is provided by the group.

Where

Sessions are held in Sweetwater Creek Nature Reserve, Frankston South. See the entrances to the reserve, and email us for the meeting point.
